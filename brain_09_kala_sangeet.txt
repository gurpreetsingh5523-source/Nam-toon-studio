🎵 7 RAGAS OF PUNJABI MUSIC:

Raga 1: BHAIRAVI (ਭੈਰਵੀ)
   Time: Early morning
   Mood: Devotional, peaceful
   Used in: Asa di Var, morning kirtan
   Feeling: "ਸਵੇਰ ਦੀ ਸ਼ਾਂਤੀ"

Raga 2: BILAWAL (ਬਿਲਾਵਲ)
   Time: Morning
   Mood: Joyful, bright
   Used in: Celebrations, happy songs
   Feeling: "ਖੁਸ਼ੀ ਦਾ ਮਾਹੌਲ"

Raga 3: MALHAR (ਮਲ੍ਹਾਰ)
   Time: Monsoon/Rain
   Mood: Romantic, longing
   Used in: Sawan songs, rain poetry
   Feeling: "ਬਰਸਾਤ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਰ"

Raga 4: DARBARI (ਦਰਬਾਰੀ)
   Time: Late night
   Mood: Serious, deep
   Used in: Sufi music, deep gurbani
   Feeling: "ਗੰਭੀਰ ਭਾਵਨਾ"

Raga 5: KAFI (ਕਾਫੀ)
   Time: Afternoon
   Mood: Soothing, romantic
   Used in: Love songs, poetry
   Feeling: "ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ"

Raga 6: YAMAN (ਯਮਨ)
   Time: Evening
   Mood: Peaceful, spiritual
   Used in: Evening kirtan, ghazals
   Feeling: "ਸ਼ਾਮ ਦਾ ਸੁਕੂਨ"

Raga 7: BHOPALI (ਭੋਪਾਲੀ)
   Time: Any time (flexible)
   Mood: Simple, folk
   Used in: Folk songs, children songs
   Feeling: "ਸਰਲ ਸੁਰ"

💡 Understand 7 ragas → Feel ANY Punjabi music!

SECTION 2: PUNJABI POETRY (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਕਵਿਤਾ)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📜 10 POETRY PATTERNS:

Pattern 1: DOHRA (ਦੋਹਰਾ)
   24 matras per line, 2 lines
   Example: "ਪਹਿਲਾ ਵਸੈ ਸਚੁ ਮਨਿ, ਸਾਚੇ ਬਾਣੀ ਸੋਇ"
   Use: Short wisdom, quick message

Pattern 2: CHAUPAI (ਚੌਪਈ)
   16 matras per line, rhyming
   Used in: Dasam Granth, storytelling
   "ਚਾਰ ਲੀਨਾਂ, ਇੱਕ ਕਹਾਣੀ"

Pattern 3: KABIT (ਕਬਿੱਤ)
   Complex meter, descriptive
   Used in: Bhai Gurdas, vivid imagery
   "ਗਹਿਰੀ ਕਵਿਤਾ, ਸੁੰਦਰ ਸ਼ਬਦ"

Pattern 4: SLOK (ਸਲੋਕ)
   Flexible meter, philosophical
   Used in: SGGS, deep meanings
   "ਗਿਆਨ ਦੀਆਂ ਗੱਲਾਂ"

Pattern 5: VAAR (ਵਾਰ)
   Heroic ballad style
   Used in: History, battles, bravery
   "ਬਹਾਦਰੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਕਹਾਣੀਆਂ"

Pattern 6: GHAZAL (ਗ਼ਜ਼ਲ)
   Rhyming couplets, romantic
   Used in: Love poetry, Sufi
   "ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਭਾਸ਼ਾ"

Pattern 7: RUBAI (ਰੁਬਾਈ)
   4 lines, AABA rhyme
   Used in: Short poems, wisdom
   "ਛੋਟੀ ਜਿਹੀ, ਗਹਿਰੀ ਗੱਲ"

Pattern 8: MASNAVI (ਮਸਨਵੀ)
   Long narrative poem
   Used in: Epic stories
   "ਲੰਮੀ ਕਹਾਣੀ"

Pattern 9: TARANA (ਤਰਾਨਾ)
   Rhythmic, musical
   Used in: Songs, celebrations
   "ਗਾਉਣ ਵਾਲੀ ਕਵਿਤਾ"

Pattern 10: BOLIYA (ਬੋਲੀਆਂ)
   Call-response, folk
   Used in: Weddings, festivals
   "ਤਿਉਹਾਰ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ"

SECTION 3: PAINTING & VISUAL ARTS (ਪੇਂਟਿੰਗ)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎨 5 ART PRINCIPLES (Universal):

Principle 1: BALANCE
   Visual weight distribution
   Symmetric or asymmetric
   "ਸੰਤੁਲਨ = ਸੁੰਦਰਤਾ"

Principle 2: CONTRAST
   Light vs dark, big vs small
   Creates visual interest
   "ਫਰਕ ਦਿਖਾਓ = ਧਿਆਨ ਖਿੱਚੋ"

Principle 3: HARMONY
   Colors/elements work together
   Pleasing to eye
   "ਮੇਲ = ਸ਼ਾਂਤੀ"

Principle 4: MOVEMENT
   Guide viewer's eye
   Flow through composition
   "ਅੱਖਾਂ ਦੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ"

Principle 5: EMPHASIS
   Focal point, what stands out
   Where eye goes first
   "ਮੁੱਖ ਨੁਕਤਾ"

Color Theory (ਰੰਗ ਵਿਗਿਆਨ):
- Primary: Red, Blue, Yellow
- Secondary: Mix 2 primaries
- Complementary: Opposite on wheel
- Warm colors: Energy (red, orange, yellow)
- Cool colors: Calm (blue, green, purple)

Punjabi Art Forms:
- Phulkari: Embroidery art
- Miniature paintings
- Folk art (tractors, trucks)
- Henna designs
"ਰੰਗ ਨਾਲ ਭਾਵਨਾ ਦਿਖਾਓ!"

SECTION 4: STORYTELLING (ਕਹਾਣੀ ਸੁਣਾਉਣਾ)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📖 3-ACT STORY STRUCTURE:

Act 1: SETUP (Beginning)
   - Introduce character
   - Show normal life
   - Problem appears
   Punjabi: "ਇੱਕ ਵਾਰ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ ਹੈ..."

Act 2: CONFRONTATION (Middle)
   - Character struggles
   - Tries solutions
   - Things get worse
   Punjabi: "ਫਿਰ ਇਹ ਹੋਇਆ..."

Act 3: RESOLUTION (End)
   - Final battle/choice
   - Problem solved
   - New normal
   Punjabi: "ਅੰਤ ਵਿੱਚ..."

Story Elements:
1. Character: Who (ਕੌਣ)
2. Setting: Where/When (ਕਿੱਥੇ/ਕਦੋਂ)
3. Conflict: Problem (ਸਮੱਸਿਆ)
4. Plot: Events (ਕੀ ਹੋਇਆ)
5. Theme: Message (ਸੰਦੇਸ਼)
6. Resolution: Ending (ਅੰਤ)

Punjabi Storytelling Style:
- Start with hook ("ਸੁਣੋ ਇੱਕ ਗੱਲ")
- Use dialogue
- Add emotions
- Moral at end
- "ਸਿੱਖਿਆ: ..."

JHUMAR (ਝੁੰਮਰ):

LUDDI (ਲੁੱਡੀ):

Dialogue Writing:

SECTION 7: MUSIC THEORY (ਸੰਗੀਤ ਥਿਊਰੀ)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎼 7 NOTES (Sargam):

Sa (ਸਾ) - Do - Stable, grounding
Re (ਰੇ) - Re - Moving, hopeful  
Ga (ਗ) - Mi - Bright, joyful
Ma (ਮ) - Fa - Intense, emotional
Pa (ਪ) - Sol - Powerful, stable
Dha (ਧ) - La - Flowing, peaceful
Ni (ਨੀ) - Ti - Leading, anticipation

"Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa" = Musical alphabet!

Rhythm (Taal):
- Beat: Basic pulse (ਧੜਕਨ)
- Measure: Group of beats (ਮਾਤਰਾ)
- Tempo: Speed (ਰਫ਼ਤਾਰ)

Common Taals:
- Keharwa: 8 beats (folk, popular)
- Dadra: 6 beats (light, romantic)
- Teental: 16 beats (classical)
- Bhangra: 8 beats (Punjabi)

"ਤਾਲ = ਸੰਗੀਤ ਦੀ ਧੜਕਨ"

📸 COMPOSITION RULES:

Rule of Thirds:
   Divide frame into 9 squares
   Place subject at intersections
   More interesting than center
   "ਬੀਚੋਂ-ਬੀਚ ਨਾ ਰੱਖੋ!"

Leading Lines:
   Use lines to guide eye
   Roads, fences, rivers
   Create depth
   "ਲਾਈਨਾਂ ਲੈ ਜਾਣ"

Framing:
   Use natural frames
   Windows, doors, trees
   Focus on subject
   "ਫਰੇਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਫਰੇਮ"

Golden Hour:
   Sunrise/sunset light
   Soft, warm, beautiful
   Best time for photos
   "ਸੋਨੇ ਜਿਹੀ ਰੋਸ਼ਨੀ"

Lighting Tips:
- Natural light best
- Avoid harsh noon sun
- Shadows add drama
- Backlight for silhouette
"ਰੋਸ਼ਨੀ = ਫੋਟੋ ਦੀ ਜਾਨ"
