===== PUNJABI RECIPES (Quick) =====

CHAI (Tea):
Water 3/4 + Milk 1/4, boil
Add: tea leaves, cardamom, ginger
Sugar to taste. Boil 2 min. Strain.

PARATHA:
Atta + salt + water = dough
Roll flat, ghee, fold, roll again
Cook on tawa, both sides golden

DAL (Lentils):
Wash dal, add water 3x quantity
Turmeric + salt, pressure cook 3 whistles
Tadka: ghee, jeera, onion, tomato

SABZI (Vegetable):
Cut vegetables, wash
Oil + jeera + vegetables
Salt, turmeric, cover and cook
Garam masala at end
