## ⚠️ CURRENT ISSUE ANALYSIS

### Problem: Dialogue Playing Twice (Double Audio)

**Possible Causes:**
1. Voice Brain creates dialogue → saves to file
2. Audio Brain might also add dialogue?
3. Video clip adds audio twice?

**Solution:**
- Voice Brain: ONLY create dialogue file
- Audio Brain: ONLY background music/SFX (NO dialogue)
- Master Brain: Combine them (dialogue + background) with proper volumes
- Video Clip: Add final mixed audio ONCE

**Proper Volume Levels:**
- Dialogue: 100% (loud, clear, understandable)
- Background Music: 20-30% (quiet, don't overpower dialogue)
- Sound Effects: 40-60% (noticeable but not distracting)
- Ambient: 15-25% (subtle atmosphere)
