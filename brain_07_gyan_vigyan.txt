===== MATHEMATICS (ਗਣਿਤ) =====

CORE OPERATIONS (Everything builds from these):
+ Addition: Combine elements
- Subtraction: Remove elements  
× Multiplication: Repeated addition
÷ Division: Repeated subtraction

ALGEBRA PATTERN:
Unknown = x → Isolate x → Solve
Example: 2x + 5 = 15 → 2x = 10 → x = 5

GEOMETRY SHORTCUTS:
- Triangle: Sum of angles = 180°
- Circle: Circumference = 2πr, Area = πr²
- Pythagorean: a² + b² = c² (right triangle)

STATISTICS ESSENCE:
Data → Average (mean) → Spread (std dev) → Predict (trend)

===== PHYSICS (ਭੌਤਿਕ ਵਿਗਿਆਨ) =====

3 FUNDAMENTAL LAWS:
1. F = ma (Force = mass × acceleration)
   - More mass OR more acceleration = More force
   
2. Energy Conservation
   - Energy changes form, never destroyed
   - Kinetic ↔ Potential ↔ Heat ↔ Light
   
3. Action = Reaction
   - Push wall → Wall pushes back
   - Every force has equal opposite

MOTION PATTERN:
v = u + at (velocity = initial + acceleration×time)
s = ut + ½at² (distance formula)

ENERGY SHORTCUTS:
- Higher position = More potential energy
- Faster motion = More kinetic energy
- E = mc² (mass IS energy)

===== CHEMISTRY (ਰਸਾਇਣ ਵਿਗਿਆਨ) =====

ATOM STRUCTURE:
Center: Protons (+) + Neutrons (0)
Orbits: Electrons (-)
Pattern: Electrons determine behavior

PERIODIC TABLE LOGIC:
- Same column = Similar properties
- Left to right = Less to more reactive (non-metals)
- Metals give electrons, Non-metals take electrons

REACTION PATTERN:
Reactants → Products
Bond breaking (needs energy) + Bond making (releases energy)
Net energy decides if reaction happens

ACIDS & BASES:
- Acid = Sour, pH < 7, gives H⁺
- Base = Bitter, pH > 7, takes H⁺
- Neutral = pH = 7 (like water)

===== BIOLOGY (ਜੀਵ ਵਿਗਿਆਨ) =====

CELL = Basic unit of life
- Membrane: Boundary (controls what enters/exits)
- Nucleus: Control center (DNA instructions)
- Mitochondria: Power plant (makes energy)

CENTRAL DOGMA:
DNA → RNA → Protein
(Instructions → Message → Action)

EVOLUTION PATTERN:
Variation + Selection + Time = New species
- Helpful traits survive
- Harmful traits disappear

BODY SYSTEMS (Purpose):
- Digestive: Food → Energy
- Respiratory: O₂ in, CO₂ out
- Circulatory: Transport nutrients/oxygen
- Nervous: Control & coordination
- Excretory: Remove waste

HOMEOSTASIS:
Body maintains balance (temperature, sugar, water)
Too hot → Sweat | Too cold → Shiver

===== UNIVERSAL PROBLEM-SOLVING =====

STEP 1: IDENTIFY TYPE
- Is it calculation? → Use formula
- Is it concept? → Recall principle  
- Is it application? → Pattern match

STEP 2: KNOWN → UNKNOWN
List what you have → Find what you need

STEP 3: CONNECT
Find equation/principle that links known to unknown

STEP 4: CALCULATE
Do the math, check units

STEP 5: VERIFY
Does answer make sense? (No negative distance, etc.)
