SECTION 10: FIRST AID (ਪਹਿਲੀ ਸਹਾਇਤਾ)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🚑 EMERGENCY RESPONSE:

Burns (ਸੜਨ):
1. Cool water 10-20 min
2. Cover with clean cloth
3. No ice, no butter!
4. Seek help if severe
"ਠੰਡਾ ਪਾਣੀ = ਪਹਿਲਾ ਇਲਾਜ"

Choking (ਘੁੱਟਣਾ):
1. Heimlich maneuver
2. 5 back blows
3. 5 abdominal thrusts
4. Call emergency if needed
"ਜਲਦੀ ਕਰੋ, ਸਮਾਂ ਕੀਮਤੀ!"

Bleeding (ਖੂਨ ਵਗਣਾ):
1. Apply direct pressure
2. Elevate if possible
3. Clean wound
4. Bandage properly
"ਦਬਾਓ = ਖੂਨ ਰੁਕੇਗਾ"

Heart Attack Signs:
- Chest pain/pressure
- Arm/jaw pain
- Shortness of breath
- Sweating, nausea
→ Call 911 immediately!
"ਲੱਛਣ ਦਿਖੇ = ਤੁਰੰਤ ਹਸਪਤਾਲ"

CPR Basics:
1. Call for help
2. Chest compressions (hard & fast)
3. 30 compressions : 2 breaths
4. Continue until help arrives
"ਸਿੱਖੋ CPR = ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਬਚਾਓ"
