### ✅ Complete Punjabi Phonetics System

#### **12 Vowels (ਮਾਤਰਾਵਾਂ - Matras)**
```
ੁ  Aunkar      - Short 'u' sound
ੂ  Dulainkar   - Long 'oo' sound
ਿ  Sihari      - Short 'i' sound

ੇ  Laan        - 'e' sound

ੌ  Kanaura     - 'au' sound
ਾ  Kanna       - Long 'aa' sound
ਂ  Bindi       - Nasal marker
ੰ  Tippi       - Nasal 'ng' marker
ੱ  Addak       - Consonant doubling/stress
```

#### **33 Consonants (ਵਿਅੰਜਨ - Vianjan)**

**Velars (ਕੰਠ):** ਕ ਖ ਗ ਘ ਙ  
**Palatals (ਤਾਲੂ):** ਚ ਛ ਜ ਝ ਞ  
**Retroflexes (ਮੂਰਧਾ):** ਟ ਠ ਡ ਢ ਣ  
**Dentals (ਦੰਦ):** ਤ ਥ ਦ ਧ ਨ  
**Labials (ਓਠ):** ਪ ਫ ਬ ਭ ਮ  
**Semi-vowels (ਅਰਧ ਸਵਰ):** ਯ ਰ ਲ ਵ ੜ  
**Sibilants (ਦੰਦੀ):** ਸ ਸ਼ ਹ

### ✅ Tonal System Support

Punjabi is a **tonal language** - one of the few Indo-Aryan languages with lexical tones!

- **Level tone** - Normal pitch
- **High tone** - After voiced aspirates (ਘ, ਝ, ਢ, ਧ, ਭ)
- **Low tone** - After h-deletion
- **Rising tone** - Upward pitch change
- **Falling tone** - Downward pitch change

## 🎵 MUSIC INTELLIGENCE

### Indian Classical Ragas (6 Types)
```
Raag Bhairav    - Morning devotional, serious mood
Raag Yaman      - Evening peaceful, romantic
Raag Darbari    - Late night, profound serious
Raag Malkauns   - Night meditation
Raag Asa        - Early morning prayer (Gurbani)
Raag Bhupali    - Evening joyful, light
```

### Taal Patterns (4 Rhythms)
```
Teental   - 16 beats (4+4+4+4) - Classical kirtan
Keharwa   - 8 beats (4+4) - Folk, light music
Dadra     - 6 beats (3+3) - Light devotional
Jhaptal   - 10 beats (2+3+2+3) - Gurbani kirtan
```

### Tempo Intelligence
- **Very Slow** - 40 BPM (meditation, grief, prayer)
- **Slow** - 60 BPM (sad, devotional, peaceful)
- **Moderate** - 90 BPM (neutral, conversation)
- **Medium** - 120 BPM (happy, dance)
- **Fast** - 150 BPM (excited, celebration, bhangra)
- **Very Fast** - 180 BPM (intense, climax)

## 🙏 GURBANI KIRTAN MASTERY

### Kirtan Styles (3 Types)

#### 1. **Classical Style**
- **Raags:** Asa, Darbari, Bhairav
- **Instruments:** Harmonium, Tabla, Tanpura
- **Tempo:** Slow, meditative
- **Ornamentation:** High (meend, gamak, kan, murki)

#### 2. **Raagi Style** (Traditional Gurdwara)
- **Raags:** Asa, Bilaval, Maru
- **Instruments:** Harmonium, Tabla
- **Tempo:** Moderate
- **Ornamentation:** Medium

#### 3. **Hazoori Style** (Court/Official)
- **Raags:** Traditional Gurbani scales
- **Instruments:** Harmonium, Tabla, Jori
- **Tempo:** Moderate, dignified
- **Ornamentation:** Low

### Vocal Ornamentation Techniques
- **Meend (मींड)** - Smooth glide between notes
- **Gamak (गमक)** - Oscillation/vibrato
- **Kan (कण)** - Grace notes
- **Murki (मुर्की)** - Quick decorative turn
